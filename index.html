Last Letter For You

Twenty-two pages. One goodbye. Written by me, of course and it fill with everything I didn't get the chance to say it.

Page 2

Dear Mrs. Nanas🍍

This might be the last letter I ever send you. I don't know if you'll read to the end, or write back — maybe that was never the point. I only wanted to say what's been sitting, unfinished, inside me.

Page 3

Everything I Never Said

If we had still together, I’d probably be talking your ear off right now with everything I’ve been wanting to tell you. There are so many random things I wanted to share—little details about my days, stuff happening in my life, things I wanted to ask you, and even silly little things that don't really matter, but for some reason, I just wanted to tell you about them. Because back then, you were my go-to person for everything. And honestly, that’s been one of the hardest parts about losing you: it wasn't just losing someone I love, but losing the person I used to share my whole world with. .

Page 4

News From Cikarang

Oh, by the way, there’s actually something I wanted to tell you! I’m in Cikarang now! I got here on Friday the 14th, so today’s my 5th day here. I haven't started my internship just yet. For now, I'm just taking a mini breather in Cikarang before heading over to Cimahi on the 29th for my campus internship. It’s kind of funny thinking about it. Before, this is the exact kind of update I would've texted you right away without even thinking twice:

  • "Nassss, aku lah di cikarang.!"
  • "Nass dijalan tadi aku sobok urang random"
  • "Nassss, akawkao tambah jauah LDR wak nas."

And from there, we’d probably get lost in some long, endless conversation about random things.

Page 5

Life Goes On

Now, I can only put all of this in a letter. I don't know if you still care to know what's going on in my life, but I still wanted to give you a little update anyway. I'm doing okay. I’m taking things day by day. And I’m trying to step into a new chapter. I guess this is also my way of telling you that even though this farewell still feels heavy, my life is still moving forward. I’m starting my internship soon, learning new things, meeting new people, and slowly rebuilding my life piece by piece. It truly feels like a new chapter in my life.

Page 6

I Still Miss You

I still miss you. I still care about you deeply. And if I just blindly followed my heart, I’d tell you that I still want you back. I still wish we had another shot to fix things. A part of me keeps hoping that maybe someday you’ll come back, and we can give it another go as more mature versions of ourselves who understand each other better. But whenever I listen to my head, I realize I probably need to accept that this farewell was really for the best for you. And honestly? I'm still caught in the middle, trying to figure out which one to follow— My heart, or my head.

Page 7

Why We Ended

Deep down in my heart, I know one thing for sure: I made mistakes in all of this. Our relationship didn't end because of someone else. Nobody came along and stole you away from me. It was all on me—I was just too careless. I was insensitive, and I failed to truly understand how you felt. There were things I should have caught onto much sooner. Things you were feeling that I just didn't grasp the weight of at the time. There were moments where I should have listened better, paid closer attention, and understood you without waiting until it was too late.

Page 8

If Only

And that's what I regret the most. Sometimes I can't help but think:

• "If only I had understood you better back then."

• "If only I had been more sensitive."

• "If only I had noticed sooner."

Maybe things would be totally different right now. Maybe we'd still be together. Maybe I’d still have my favorite person to share all these thoughts with. But the reality is, we can't rewind time.

Page 9

The Regret That Stays

And I think that's the hardest pill to swallow. There's this lingering regret that might never fully fade away. It’ll probably always sit in a quiet corner of my heart, like a little reminder of something that used to mean the absolute world to me. But I don't want to spend my life constantly blaming myself. I want to learn from all of this. Learn how to love someone with more empathy, how to really listen, how to appreciate the little details, and how to value someone while they’re still by my side, not after they're gone

Page 10

A Grain Of Sand

I remember telling you that love never truly fades away—even if, in the end, only a single grain of sand remains amidst a vast desert. And now I understand—what I said back then was true. Perhaps these feelings will change. Maybe one day, it won’t hurt as much as it does now. Maybe one day, I’ll be able to think of you without that tightness in my chest. But I don’t want to pretend I no longer care about you just because I’m trying to let you go. I still love you. And a small part of that love might stay with me forever.

Page 11

Why I Blocked You

I also wanted to clear the air about why I blocked you on social media. It wasn't out of hate. Not even a little bit. I just know myself too well. I honestly don't know how I'd handle seeing you with someone new down the road. I don't know how deeply it would sting to see you smiling, happy, and living your life with someone who isn't me. I just wasn't sure if I was strong enough to watch that. So I chose to step away from it.

Page 12

Not A Punishment

I didn't remove myself from your feed to punish you, or to make you look for me, or to make you feel guilty. I was just trying to protect my own peace of mind. I’m keeping this one small door open through this email address. Not because I'm sitting around waiting for you, or to make you feel like you should come back. But just in case life brings us to different places in the future, maybe we can talk again when we're both in a much better place❤️.

Page 13

If You Ever Come Back

If you were to come back into my life someday, honestly, I don’t know if I could bring myself to say no—in fact, I probably never could. My heart might still remain open to welcoming you back. Because, to be frank, if that chance arose again, I think I would still want to give it another try—though I suspect you wouldn't want to repeat it. Nevertheless, I won’t put my life on hold waiting for that day. I don’t want my world to stop turning just because of the slim possibility that you might return. If fate brings us together again, we’ll just have to wait and see what happens.

Page 14

Not Just Friends

We never gave us an official title, but please know it was never just a casual friendship to me—we genuinely loved and cared for each other in ways words couldn't fully define.

My biggest heartbreak is realizing I was blind to what you were carrying inside. I never knew how tired you were of living in that middle ground, or how much you secretly wished for us to be together properly. If only you had given me an ultimatum back then... if only you had told me, "Either we make this real, or I'm leaving," I would have chosen you without hesitation.

I would have held your hand, given you the certainty you deserved, and fought for us with everything I had. I'm so sorry I made you feel like you had to walk away just to be heard.

Page 15

All Or Nothing

If our paths ever cross again in the future, I don't want us to come back as friends. If we come back to each other, I’d want you to be fully, truly my girlfriend. But if that never happens, that's okay too. I'd much rather we stay strangers than force something that isn't meant to be anymore.

Page 16

For You And Him

Now I want to say something that's probably the hardest thing for me to write. I truly hope you're happy. I hope you and the person you're with right now find all the happiness you're looking for. I really hope he treats you and loves you better than I ever did. I hope he pays closer attention to you than I managed to. I hope he understands all the subtle, little things about you that I failed to get. I hope he treats you with all the care and tenderness you deserve.

Page 17

Your Happiness

I even hope he loves you far better than I ever knew how to. Not because I stopped caring about you— In fact, precisely because I still care, I want nothing but the absolute best for you, even if the person giving you that happiness isn't me. If your ultimate happiness isn't meant to be with me, I hope someone else gives you more joy than you ever even asked for. I just want you to be happy. Truly, genuinely happy.

Page 18

Not Hoping He Leaves

Maybe one day, the person you're with now will leave you. If that day comes, I'll welcome you back with an open heart. But I don't expect that at all. In fact, I hope the opposite. I hope they continue to love you, stay by your side, and that you build something beautiful together and find true happiness. Because if I truly love you, I shouldn't be praying for your world to fall apart just to get a second chance.

Page 19

If Life Brings Us Back

But if life ever brings us back to a place where we find each other again, maybe then we can see if there's still something worth fighting for. For now, though, I'm not going to wait around for that moment. I'm going to live my life. And you should live yours, too. I'm letting you go not because my love ran out, but because I've realized that loving someone sometimes means having the courage to let them walk a path that isn't with you.

Page 20

I'm Sorry

I'm sorry for not being observant enough back then. I'm sorry for all the things I should have done but didn't. I'm sorry that I only fully understood so many things after it was all over. I can't change the past. But I promise myself that I'll take every single lesson from our time together to become a better person. Not just for whoever comes into my life next, But for myself, too.

Page 21

Thank You

Thank you for being a part of my life.

Thank you for being my soulmate, for being my home, and for being the biggest source of my happiness.

Thank you for all the memories and times we shared.

Thank you for all the stories we got to share with each other.

Thank you for being someone who meant the absolute world to me.

I probably won't ever be able to erase all my memories of you.

And honestly, I don't think I need to.

I’ll let them remain a precious part of my journey. I just need to learn how not to live in them anymore.

Goodbye

Take care of yourself. Find your happiness. I loved you so much. I still love you — and some small part of that will probably always remain.

I hope you're happy.
— Me
