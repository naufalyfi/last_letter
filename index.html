Last Letter For You

Twenty-two pages. One goodbye. Written somewhere between Cikarang and everything I never said.

To You

This might be the last letter I ever send you. I don't know if you'll read to the end, or write back — maybe that was never the point. I only wanted to say what's been sitting, unfinished, inside me.

Everything I Never Said

If we hadn't drifted apart, I'd probably still be talking your ear off — small stories, saved-up questions, things too ordinary for anyone but you. Losing you meant losing the one place I kept my stories.

News From Cikarang

I'm in Cikarang now — arrived Friday the 14th. Internship starts in Cimahi on the 29th. I would've texted you all of this without thinking twice. Now I can only write it here.

Life Goes On

I don't know if you still want to know how I'm doing — but I want you to, anyway. I'm okay. Learning things, meeting people, slowly building something again.

I Still Miss You

My heart says come back — older, gentler, understanding each other better this time. My mind says this ending was right for you. I'm still caught between the two.

Why We Ended

No one came between us. It was me — too careless, too slow to notice what you carried. Things I should have understood before it was too late to matter.

If Only

If only I'd understood sooner.
If only I'd paid attention.
If only I'd woken up in time.

Maybe we'd still be together. But we can't go back.

The Regret That Stays

Some regrets never fully leave — they stay in a small corner of me. But I don't want to live by blaming myself. I want to learn to notice someone before they're gone.

A Grain Of Sand

I once said love doesn't disappear, even shrunk to one grain of sand against the whole universe. I still love you. Some part of that will probably always stay.

Why I Blocked You

Not hatred — not even close. I just know myself. I don't know how deep it would cut to see you happy with someone else, so I chose not to look.

Not A Punishment

This isn't to punish you, or make you chase me. I'm saving myself. I'll keep one small path open — through email — just in case life brings us somewhere better.

If You Ever Come Back

I don't know if I could turn you away. Some part of my heart would still open that door. But I won't wait. If life brings us back, we'll see what happens then.

Not Just Friends

I can't pretend we're ordinary friends. We loved each other — forcing anything less would feel like lying to my own heart.

All Or Nothing

If we come back, I want you back fully — truly mine again. If that never happens, that's alright too. Better strangers than something we can't really live in.

For You And Him

The hardest thing to write: I hope you're happy. I hope he understands the small things about you I failed to. I hope he treats you better than I did.

Your Happiness

I hope he loves you better than I ever could — not because I've stopped caring, but because I still do. I just want you truly happy.

Not Hoping He Leaves

I don't wish for him to leave. I hope the opposite — that he stays, that you find what you're looking for. Real love doesn't hope for someone else's happiness to fall apart.

If Life Brings Us Back

Maybe someday we'll find out if there's still something worth fighting for. For now, I won't wait around. I'll live my life. You should live yours.

I'm Sorry

Sorry for not being attentive enough. Sorry I only understood after it ended. I can't change the past — but I'll carry what this taught me and become someone better.

Thank You

Thank you for being part of my life, for every story we shared, for once mattering so much. I won't erase these memories — I'll just learn not to live inside them.

If I Become A Stranger

I hope you don't remember me only for my mistakes. I hope you remember someone who loved you completely, who only ever wanted your happiness.

Goodbye

Take care of yourself. Find your happiness. I loved you so much. I still love you — and some small part of that will probably always remain.

I hope you're happy.
— Me
